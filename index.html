About

A public space for art

The idea is simple: art should be easy to find, easy to buy and fair to the people who make it. My Public Space shows a small, carefully chosen selection instead of an endless catalogue.

Every work is described honestly, with its medium, size and edition. If you want to know more about a piece or an artist, write to us and we will answer personally.
